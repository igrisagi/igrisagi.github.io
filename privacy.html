Privacy

ErenLabs stores only what is needed to run this site: your display name, sign-in details, messages you send, and comments you post. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes; sessions use HttpOnly cookies.

The visitor counter records a daily, anonymized hash of each visitor (IP plus user agent, hashed, never stored in raw form) solely to count qualified visits.

Private messages are visible only to you and the site administrator. Nothing here is sold or shared with third parties.
