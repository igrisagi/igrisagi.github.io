A small corner of the web for engineering notes, experiments, and slow progress toward AI research.

Eren Labs is the personal site of an electronics and communication engineering graduate from Tamil Nadu, India, now spending most days learning machine learning properly - the math, the code, and the papers - with the long-term goal of doing research that matters.

About

I graduated in electronics and communication engineering, which means I came to software the long way around: signals, circuits, and a lot of applied math first. That turned out to be useful. The same math sits underneath modern machine learning, and I enjoy connecting the two.

Right now this site is mostly a public notebook and a placeholder for bigger things. I would rather put up something plain and honest than a polished page with nothing behind it.
